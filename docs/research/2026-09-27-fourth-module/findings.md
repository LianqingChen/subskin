## Recommendation and uncertainty

- Preserve hospital information and patient experiences, but treat independent top-level placement as unproven.
- Preferred hypothesis under growth/retention priority: 计划 / 我的计划, a patient-controlled action workflow connecting Q&A, records, consultation preparation and optional community sharing.
- Alternative 1: expand 就医; useful for acquisition/episodic needs, but needs maintained local information and sufficient community contributions.
- Alternative 2: small peer-support groups; potentially useful for belonging but overlaps 分享 and requires moderation/community operations.
- Alternative 3: service marketplace; potential transaction revenue but premature without supply, trust and retention.
- Start with a plan card inside existing experience and a small voluntary cohort; promote to fourth navigation only after repeated action completion demonstrates distinct value.
- No medical treatment plans generated by AI. Tasks come from user goals or user-confirmed clinician instructions. No automatic dosing or phototherapy adjustment.
- No automatic publication, third-party access or enrolling users in sponsored programs. Explicit scoped authorization remains necessary.
- Monetization sequence: free core retention loop; optional advanced organization; later institution-paid authorized follow-up and disclosed patient support projects. These are business hypotheses, not existing revenue capabilities.
- No current production analytics, financial data or patient records inspected. Historical September 17 design noted seven hospitals and zero experience content; this must not be described as current counts.

## Validation design

- First inspect de-identified aggregate funnel: first useful answer, first saved record, second useful action, hospital search success and return patterns.
- Interview 10–15 users with different stages, including early drop-offs. Pilot with 20–30 volunteers for approximately 6–8 weeks; extend if their next intended record/visit has not occurred.
- Evaluate first-week activation and second-cycle completion separately. The denominator for on-time completion is users whose self-selected task is due, not all signups.
- Compare with the current flow and acquisition-source mix; use randomized comparison only if sample size supports it. Small cohorts offer qualitative direction, not proof of causal lift.
- Measure record save failures/time-to-value and opt-outs alongside retention. Do not optimize daily check-ins or more frequent photographs.
- If benefit is only a reminder or use is limited to consultation days, keep it embedded in records rather than forcing a fourth top-level module.
